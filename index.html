Drop. Merge. Watermelon!

Drop fruit into the bin. When two matching fruits touch, they merge into the next one up — cherry to strawberry, grape to orange, all the way to the mighty watermelon. Chain your merges, ride the danger line, and see how long you can survive before the bin overflows.
