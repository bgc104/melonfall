Common questions

Do I need an account or a sign-in?
No. Melonfall has no accounts. The Game Center leaderboard is optional and uses your Apple Game Center identity.
Does it work offline?
Yes — the full game is playable offline. Ads and the leaderboard need a connection.
How do the ads work?
An ad may appear at game-over, never mid-game. You're also offered an optional rewarded ad you can choose to watch for a one-time continue. You never have to watch one to keep playing.
How is my score submitted?
If you're signed in to Game Center, your high score is submitted under your Game Center identity. If you're not signed in, nothing is sent.
What's the Watermelon Counter?
Most players never make a watermelon. The game shows a live count of how many Game Center players ever have — and when you make your first one, you get your maker number. It's rarer than it looks.
Why did the bin end my run?
When fruit stacks above the danger line and stays there, the run ends. Merge pairs to keep the stack down — and chase the watermelon.
