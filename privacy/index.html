Privacy Policy

Melonfall for iPhone · Effective date: October 26, 2026

The short version

Melonfall has no accounts, no server of its own, and no analytics of its own. The game itself does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or purchase history.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Melonfall shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, the AdMob SDK may collect:

  • Identifiers — your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), used for third-party advertising.
  • Usage data — ad interactions (such as ad views and taps), used for third-party advertising.

On iOS, the App Tracking Transparency prompt is shown before this data is used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, ads still appear but are not personalized in that way. Google's handling of this data is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Game Center

The leaderboards are Apple's Game Center. If you're signed in, your high score — and, if you make a watermelon, your watermelon count — are submitted under your Game Center identity and handled by Apple. If you're not signed in, no score leaves your device.

What stays on your device

Game progress and your local high score are stored on your iPhone only.

Children

Melonfall is a casual puzzle game with no chat, no user-generated content, and no direct collection of personal information by the game itself.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: see the support page.
